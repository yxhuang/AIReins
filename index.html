Your browser. Your computer.

One Node process. The official CLIs. Your existing logins.

On the same computer, your browser connects directly to loopback. From another device, join your tailnet: Tailscale Serve forwards to AIReins’ remote entry, which checks Host/Origin and allows only the Tailscale logins you approve. The local Node process drives the four official CLIs over stream-json, ACP and codex app-server.

Read Security first.

This is access to your whole computer.

Anyone who can reach AIReins has the power of your user account on that machine.

  • Loopback onlyAIReins listens on the local loopback address.
